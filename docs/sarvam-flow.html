Coming back later

If you’ve met this person before, the game remembers—and Sarvam helps with a warm welcome before the lesson continues.

Sarvam chat — short “remember you?” greeting → Sarvam text-to-speech — speaks it aloud ↗ joins the listen step above

Where Sarvam shows up

  • Speech-to-text — turns your spoken attempt into words the game checks against the lesson phrase.
  • Text-to-speech — makes locals sound natural in the district’s language.
  • Chat — writes a short, personal greeting when you revisit someone you’ve met before.

Behind the scenes

  • Lesson lines can be pre-recorded with Sarvam text-to-speech ahead of time so replies feel instant when you play.
  • On-screen help in your reading language is prepared offline with Sarvam chat, then looked up in-game—no live translation while you play.

There is also a live free-talk voice experience in the project that is not connected to the current lesson screen yet.

SADAK is a street-language learning game. Sarvam provides speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and chat—the game handles scoring and progress.
