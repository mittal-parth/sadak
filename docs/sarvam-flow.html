Coming back later

If you’ve talked to this person before, the game remembers—and Sarvam helps with a warm welcome.

Sarvam chat writes a short “remember you?” greeting → Sarvam text-to-speech speaks it aloud

Where Sarvam shows up

Speech-to-text

Turns your spoken attempt into words the game can check against the lesson phrase.

Text-to-speech

Makes locals sound natural when they talk to you in the district’s language.

Chat

Writes a short, personal greeting when you revisit someone you’ve met before.

Behind the scenes

  • Lesson lines can be pre-recorded with Sarvam text-to-speech ahead of time so replies feel instant when you play.
  • On-screen help in your reading language is prepared offline with Sarvam chat, then looked up during the game—no live translation while you play.

Note

There is also a live free-talk voice experience in the project that is not connected to the current lesson screen yet.

SADAK is a street-language learning game. Sarvam provides speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and chat—the game handles scoring and progress.
